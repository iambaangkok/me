## Context

I'm going to Japan to visit my partner over the end-of-year break. I wanted to bring something made, not just bought, so from the start of December I was pretty busy with CAD.

## Branch ring

A branch ring design with organic-ish lines, printed in grey, painted with metallic copper. Made 2! one for me and one for her.

![branch ring](</image/3dprints/003 branch-ring.jpg#small>)

## Earrings

Rhombus earrings:

![kite earrings](</image/3dprints/004 kite-earrings.jpg#small>)

And **Star and Cross Tiling** / infinite tiling inspired earrings. My partner is doing her master's at JAIST (Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology) and her research touches tiling problems, so this one felt on-theme.

![tiling-inspired earrings](</image/3dprints/005 infinite-tiling-earrings.jpg#small>)

If you want to see what she's up to academically, her site is here: <a href="https://ponpailinh.github.io/me/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">ponpailinh.github.io/me</a>

## 2025 Love Struck Seal of Purity

Last piece: a silly-but-serious "2025 Love Struck Seal of Purity," basically an award for surviving the year and grinding on research. Printed, painted a bit, meant to make her laugh and also feel seen.

![love struck seal of purity](</image/3dprints/006 love-stuck-seal-of-purity.jpg#small>)

## That's the batch

Hope she likes them. (She already told me she likes them hahahaha). If not I'll eat the filament costs and try again next year 😅
