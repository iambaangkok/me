## More parts

21 Nov 2025. I've been cranking out new tank parts since the last log. The pile on my desk is starting to look like an actual kit instead of just test pins.

This batch includes:

* Engine parts
* Hull sections
* Track pieces
* A few armor variants
* A few gun variants

…and a little seat for Dororo-kun because if I'm going to stare at CAD for hours, at least someone's enjoying the ride.
